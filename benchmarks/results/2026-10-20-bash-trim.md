# Trim passing runner output to its tail

Tested on 2026-10-20 as a $0 replay of recorded real-use transcripts. The script and notes are local and untracked in the main checkout: `.exo/self-improve/tests/s5/bash-trim.mjs` and `.exo/self-improve/s5-iter6.md`.

## Method (pre-registered)

A passing project command (test, build, check, lint, proof) leaves only its last 20 lines plus a 15-token stub in the agent's context; a failing one stays in full. Only results over 400 tokens count. Saving is the cut tokens times one cache write and one cache read per later request. A penalty is subtracted when a later turn quotes a cut line, as if the agent re-read the full log. Exo's own scripts are excluded (covered by the "Script stdout contract" row). Metric is net saving over the type's cost, 95% bootstrap CI over agent sessions, 10,000 resamples. Bar: at least 3% with a CI low above 0.

## Results

| Type | Agents | Net saving, runner commands | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| build-task | 1,939 | 0.14% | [0.11, 0.17] |
| solve-hard | 201 | 0.09% | [0.04, 0.14] |
| run-unit | 315 | 0.02% | [0.01, 0.04] |
| lead | 1,017 | 0.02% | [0.02, 0.03] |

- Agents already pipe or redirect 81% of runner calls (6,127 of 7,577), so little output reaches context.
- Later turns quoted a cut line in 6.4% of trimmed results.
- With 2.17 characters per token the largest figure is 0.25% (build-task).
- Secondary upper bound, all Bash commands: 3.43% for build-task, 3.13% for solve-hard, 1.09% for run-unit; not the verdict.

Verdict: rejected, far below the 3% bar.
