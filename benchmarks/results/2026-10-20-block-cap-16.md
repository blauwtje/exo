# Raise the block task cap from 8 to 16

Tested on 2026-10-20 as a $0 offline replay of recorded real-use transcripts. Scripts, prereg and notes are local and untracked in the main checkout under `.exo/self-improve/s7/`.

Prereg r3-test-H1-prereg.md (written before any replay output, unchanged). Scripts tests/H1/collect.mjs and tests/H1/replay.mjs (real `parsePlan` and `readyTasks` with `nextBlock`'s loop and the limit as a parameter, bootstrap 10000 over sessions).

## Data
- Verdict window: sessions whose first run-unit starts on or after 2026-10-09 (the budget-nudge fix): 49 sessions, 97 run-units, 24 sessions replay (at least 20 needed); 25 do not (12 name no plan, 13 name a plan gone from disk, 1 with mismatched task numbers).
- Replay validity: simulated blocks at cap 8 are 31 against 30 actual fresh dispatches in the window, 93 against 93 on the full corpus.
- Window sessions are small: 30 of 49 have 1-2 run-unit dispatches, so the cap binds in only a few plans.
- Window fit: run-unit cost = $0.020 + $0.049 per task, so the fixed cost per block F = $0.230 (with the $0.21 lead cycle). Full corpus: $0.153 + $0.035 per task, F = $0.363.

## Against the prereg
- Net saving at least 0.24% of run with a CI lower bound above 0: not met (best window point 0.32%, lower bound 0.00).
- At least 1% of run at the best cap: not met in the window (0.42%); the full corpus reaches 1.62% only without a convexity charge and 0.89% with it. No variant reaches 3% of run.
- Context guard: predicted peak over 60k for 0 of 3 merged 16-blocks in the window, but 7 of 21 (33%, limit 10%) on the full corpus. Observed 8-task run-units already peak at 78k at most in the window (107k in the full corpus).
- Design guard holds by construction of `nextBlock`.

## Why it fails
Few plans in the window exceed 8 tasks (31 blocks, 3 removed). At the measured post-10-09 fixed cost the saving is under 0.5% of run and negative if the lead cycle is not credited. The earlier 3.4% upper bound came from the full corpus, where the run-unit intercept was $0.15 and plans were longer.

Verdict: FAIL, reject. No branch, no paid arm. Only the block-merge route of wake coalescing is covered; wake coalescing itself stays unproven. Not reached by this offline proof: dependency breaks beyond what `nextBlock` encodes, and the lead cost per extra cycle after 10-09 (the $0.21 comes from an earlier measurement). Ledger suggestion: `raise-the-block-task-cap-from-8-to-16` rej, 0.32-0.42% of run (CI lower bound 0.00).
