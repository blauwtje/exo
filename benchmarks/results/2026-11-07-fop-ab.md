# find-open-points ceiling at high (fop-no-raise)

Tested on 2026-11-07 as a paid A/B with `benchmarks/agent-tier.mjs --agent find-open-points --tier full`, 8 brief fixtures (`benchmarks/agent-tier/find-open-points/`, b1 to b8), one run per cell. Scripts, transcripts and grades are local and untracked under `.exo/fop-ab/` in the worktree.

Idea: the `open-points` kind ceiling in `lib/model-kinds.json` drops from `max` to `high`, so a max-effort session runs the open-point agent at high.

Verdict: FAIL on recall. The ceiling stays `max`.
Number: recall high 35/54 (64.8%) vs max 41/54 (75.9%), 11.1 points apart, over the 5-point guard. The cost side passes by a wide margin (47.1% of the bucket), but the rule needs both.

## Arms

| arm | cells done | recall | mean $/session | total $ | wall per session |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sonnet:high | 8 of 8 | 35/54 (64.8%) | 0.0408 | 0.33 | 13 to 22 s |
| sonnet:max | 7 of 8 | 41/54 (75.9%) | 0.3679 (mean of the 7 completed) | 2.58 | 200 to 227 s, b4 hit 240 s |

Recall with b4 excluded from both arms (secondary reading only): high 29/47 (61.7%), max 41/47 (87.2%).

## Grading

- References: `.exo/fop-ab/refs.json`, 54 points over 8 briefs, sha256 `4bb68b8b7122a3164cfc5e2c36992372bcac6ed9387e4baf6facc45b995d77f8`, checked before grading.
- Blind: `.exo/fop-ab/blind/make.mjs` took each completed session's final output from the harness record (`detail.text`, the report, and `detail.assumptions`, the assumptions file it wrote), shuffled the 15 runs with a fixed seed, and wrote `run-<id>.md` headed only by the fixture. Grades went to `grades.json` before `key.json` was opened; `unblind.mjs` computed recall.
- b4 had only one completed run, so that one cell could not be blind.
- A point counts as matched when the run raises the same open question, as an assumption, user point or `Cannot follow` line.

## Cost

- Bucket saving = (1 - 0.0408 / 0.3679) x 53.0% = 47.1%, where 53.0% is the max runs' share of the $20.76 real-use bucket. xhigh is not counted.
- Max mean uses its 7 completed sessions; errored sessions report no cost.

## Errors and causes

- max b4: wall cap. 240039 ms against the 240000 ms cap (`benchmarks/locate-recall.mjs:21`, `EXO_LOCATE_SESSION_MS`), 9 tool calls, no result line, no api_retry. Kept as an error and counted as 0 matched points.
- max b5 to b8: network failures in the first run (api_retry events, or a stalled stream with the wall timer running across sleep, 280 to 340 s). Rerun once each from a copy of the tree, all 4 completed.
- Max wall against the 240 s cap: completed 200, 202, 205, 210, 212, 214, 227 s; b4 at 240 s. 1 of 8 hit the cap, and the completed runs leave 5% to 17% headroom.

## Spend

$0.33 high, $1.09 max first run, $1.49 max rerun: $2.91 of the $10 cap. Errored cells report no cost, so the true spend is a little higher.

## Rule (pre-registered)

Set the ceiling to high only when recall_high >= recall_max - 5 points AND bucket saving >= 3%. Otherwise keep max. xhigh is only a separate reading.

Outcome: 64.8 < 75.9 - 5 = 70.9, so the recall condition fails. Ceiling stays `max`; `lib/model-kinds.json` is unchanged.

## Alternatives

- sonnet:xhigh as a separate reading: max hit the wall cap in 1 of 8 runs and finishes within 13 to 40 s of the cap, so a cheaper arm that keeps recall is worth one A/B with the same refs and guard. Not run here.
- Raise the 240 s cap for find-open-points in the harness (`benchmarks/agent-tier.mjs` passes no `timeoutMs`, so the locate default applies): would let max b4 complete and give a cleaner max reading. Not part of this rule.
- Keep high but fix its recall in the prompt: high misses reuse and compatibility points most often; a prompt change is a different idea and needs its own A/B.

## Caveats

- One run per cell; per-fixture differences of one or two points are noise at this n.
- One grader, blind to arm except for b4.
