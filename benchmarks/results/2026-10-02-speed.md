## Comparison

| Arm | Median wall (s) | Median tokens | Median cost (USD) | Median findings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main | 189.9 | 522104 | 1.260 | 0 |
| branch (`9f4446c`) | 315.2 | 546430 | 1.373 | 1 |
| branch, repaired (`7cab3a21`) | 243.1 | 494145 | 1.252 | 0 |

The repaired branch arm still does not beat main on wall time: its median is 28% above main's (243.1 s against 189.9 s), down from 66% for the first branch arm. Median tokens are 5% below main and median cost 1% below, and median review findings match main at 0. All nine runs landed 4/4 tasks and exited 0. Spread is wide (main 180 to 358 s, repaired branch 203 to 298 s, with run 3 below main's median), so three runs per arm cannot separate a gap this size from noise.

Not measured: the verify gate's wall time (the fixture's gate is `npm test`, 5 tests, so Task 2's skip has nothing to skip), Task 7's reader routing (the fixture plan has no discovery step), lead peak context (`cell-usage.mjs`), and the speed passes on a larger plan.
