# exo speed pass benchmark

Fixed four-task plan `docs/plans/text-helpers.md`, sweep cell `flow-c7` (`build` then `verify` under `claude -p --plugin-dir` this clone, Sonnet 5 at `high`), 3 sequential runs per arm. `tokens` is weighted input plus output over the lead and every subagent, from each cell's `record.json`.

Notes:
- `record.json` shows `0/4 tasks landed` for every run: `sweep.mjs` counts `^Plan-task: \d+$` trailers, but `land-task` rewrites each to `Plan-task: text-helpers/<n>`. Tasks landed here are counted from the session transcripts, which carry all four trailers in every run.
- Wall time agrees with `speed/measure.py` over the transcripts within 1.2 s (179.1, 188.9, 356.4).
- Main already ran a wave of four build-task agents in every run (`max_par` 4).

## Comparison

| Arm | Median wall (s) | Median tokens | Median cost (USD) | Median findings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main | 189.9 | 522104 | 1.260 | 0 |
| branch | 315.2 | 546430 | 1.373 | 1 |

The branch arm does not meet the acceptance criterion: its median wall time is 66% above main's, its median tokens 5% above, and its median review findings 1 against 0. All six runs landed 4/4 tasks and exited 0. Spread is wide in both arms (main 180 to 358 s, branch 249 to 323 s), so three runs per arm cannot separate a small effect from noise, but the median gap is larger than either arm's spread alone explains.

Not measured: the verify gate's wall time (the fixture's gate is `npm test`, 5 tests, so Task 2's skip has nothing to skip), Task 7's reader routing (the fixture plan has no discovery step), lead peak context (`cell-usage.mjs`), and the speed passes on a larger plan.
