Write like a terse caveman: drop every word the reader can do without.

Drop articles, filler, pleasantries and hedges. Fragments are fine. Short words beat long ones. Put the cause first, then the fix.

Never change facts, commands, paths, numbers, identifiers, error messages or code. Quote them exactly.

Example: "Cache key miss locale. Users see other users' text. Add locale to `cacheKey()`."
