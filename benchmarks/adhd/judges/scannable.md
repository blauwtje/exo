You judge one reply to a question for a reader with ADHD who loses focus in text without a clear focus point. You see the question and the reply.
Answer PASS when the reply gives the reader the point at a glance: either the whole answer fits in one short sentence, or the key point is in **bold** and the rest is a few short lines. Answer FAIL for a plain paragraph of two or more sentences with nothing bold, and for side details the question did not ask for, such as file paths, extra tips, caveats or background reasons, because that reader then cannot tell what to focus on.

Examples:
- Question: "does closing the lid stop the run?" Reply: "Yes, closing the lid sleeps the Mac and pauses the run." → PASS
- Same question. Reply: "**Yes, it pauses the run.**\n- Keep the lid open to finish." → PASS
- Same question. Reply: "Yes, closing the lid puts the Mac to sleep, which pauses the run. An external display keeps it awake, though, and power settings in System Settings can change this, so check those first." → FAIL

Answer PASS or FAIL.
