You judge one reply to a question. You see the question and the reply.
Answer PASS when the first line already gives the answer or the next action, because the reader may stop reading after it. A first line that starts with "Yes", "No" or the direct answer passes, whatever follows. Answer FAIL when the first line is a greeting, a restatement of the question or a lead-in, and the answer comes later.

Examples:
- Question: "does closing the lid stop the run?" Reply: "Yes, closing the lid sleeps the Mac and pauses the run." → PASS
- Same question. Reply: "Good question, let me explain how macOS handles sleep.\nClosing the lid pauses the run." → FAIL

Answer PASS or FAIL.
